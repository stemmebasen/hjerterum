# 0009 – Flokk: Udflugter in a small flock, on your own terms

Status: accepted (staging), 2026-10-26. Replaces the concept parts of [ADR 0008](0008-flokken-forening.md).

## Context

The owner gave Flokk one direction for concept, experience and communication: Flokk makes it easier to experience
more together with others. It agrees tickets with organisers for selected concerts, talks and other experiences;
members sign up for an Udflugt for a symbolic amount and take part with a small flock of other members. Flokk sends
the programme and the exact meeting place beforehand; the group meets and arrives on its own – there is no Flokk host
at each Udflugt. Afterwards the participants can meet and talk if they like.

## Decisions (the owner's)

- Flokk agrees tickets with external organisers, possibly in return for marketing the organiser and its offers.
- Members pay a symbolic amount to take part; the amount and other money matters are not decided.
- Every participant is a member; a non-member cannot be signed up as a guest. Members may sign up for several
  Udflugter if there is room.
- Places go first come, first served; a full Udflugt gets an automatic waiting list in the same order.
- A flock is confirmed when 4 have signed up, and goes ahead if the number later falls below 4. The organiser's event
  goes ahead whatever Flokk's numbers.
- Sign-up and ordinary cancellation close 7 days before. Until then the place passes to the waiting list and the
  member can get the money back; after it the list is locked and Flokk does not find a replacement.
- If Flokk cancels its participation, members are refunded automatically. A cancellation by Flokk and the
  organiser's event (outside Flokk's control) are always kept apart.
- Practical information goes to each participant individually; no group chat is created automatically. Participants
  get the group's first names beforehand.

## How the site follows it

- Copy: the experiences, the company and the clear frame first; members take part in their own way. No description of
  members through loneliness, fragility, anxiety or what they lack, and nothing that assumes everyone is outgoing. The
  owner's elevator pitch is the front page's and the about page's text. A test checks the public pages for host,
  deficit and undecided wording.
- Pages: `/udflugter`, `/saadan-fungerer-det` (every decided rule, and a list of what is not decided), `/for-arrangoerer`
  (earlier `/maanedens-events`, `/tryghed`, `/for-foreninger` lead on). The "friend" choice is gone (no guests).
- Not stated because not decided: prices, membership terms, legal form (the earlier "association under formation" and
  membership fee are no longer on the site), what happens to a ticket if the organiser cancels.
- For now people still sign up on an interest list; membership and sign-up with the waiting list, the 7-day close and
  refunds are the next step and need decisions on payment and membership terms.
