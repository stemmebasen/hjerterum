Would you like to treat yourself to a wealth of wonderful holistic, spiritual and heart-opening events and workshops? Would you like to be part of the Hjerterum community and association and enjoy the many benefits?

**Benefits of membership**:

-   As a member, you can participate in events at a discounted price and often completely free.
-   You regularly receive offers on our own events, such as concerts, lectures, theme evenings, communal dining, film evenings, etc. as part of Hjerterum ‘Open’ Events. It is often free or at a special reduced price for our members.
-   You can participate in **Tribes** 🔥  
    At Hjerterum, you can become part of a tribe, which is a small group of 5-8 participants. Tribes exist to create nurturing communities that can create meaning and coherence in the individual’s life. It is a unique opportunity to get to know yourself and other people more deeply, and achieve a meaningful and confidential connection in a small and safe community. Please write to us at [**tribes@holistisk.org**](mailto:tribes@holistisk.org) if you would like to join a **Tribe**.

-   It is easier to book events. When you are logged in to the website, you do not need to enter your name or email address.
-   You can enjoy organic coffee and other specialty drinks as well as biodynamic cakes / snacks in the café at a discount every Thursday afternoon and for other events.
-   You receive offers on events at Hjerterum’s partners in Aarhus and Eastern Jutland.
-   You have the opportunity to participate in the association with contributions, ideas and wishes.
-   You support and raise Hjerterum financially, so that together we can continue to live out the vision of creating a heart space for healing, connectedness and inner peace.
