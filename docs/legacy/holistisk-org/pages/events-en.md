At **Hjerterum** we wish to offer a variety of events, suited for everyone and ranging broadly in taste. Yoga, mindfulness, meditation, rebirthing, breathwork, dreamwork, acupuncture, chanting, mantra songs, talks, courses, community eating and film evenings are all part of the program.

This wide range of events all have the purpose, each with their unique approach, of letting you get in touch with the core of who you really are – an inner journey of discovery into being.

Click the titles below to read more about each individual event. If it is not possible to sign up for a particular event of interest, it is because all available places for that event have already been booked, or that the registration takes place in another way (e.g. by email or via a link to another website.).

If you have a good idea for a new event within the category of spirituality/self-development, then don’t hesitate to send us an email at [events@holistisk.org](mailto:events@holistisk.org)

**Photography and video recording policy** (expand)

Ask before filming and photographing – and respect a no.

In Hjerterum, we generally do not take out our phones to film or photograph. If you still want to do so, you must have clear and explicit consent from everyone involved and tell them what the material will be used for.

You always have the right to withdraw your consent and ask that pictures or videos in which you are included be deleted.

At concert nights, pictures and videos may be taken that capture the atmosphere in the room. If individuals are clearly included in the pictures or video, you always ask first – and of course respect a no.

In Hjerterum, we look after each other’s safety, privacy and trust – also digitally.
