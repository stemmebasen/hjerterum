### Viewing your participant list, editing, cancelling event etc.

1.  Login from the top menu – ask for a new password if you have forgotten yours
2.  Select ‘Calendar’ from the ‘Events’ menu
3.  Click on your event
4.  Select ‘Admin’ above your event picture – refresh page if you cannot see ‘Admin’.
5.  Select ‘Dashboard’ to see your participants.
6.  From here you can also edit all aspects of your event.
