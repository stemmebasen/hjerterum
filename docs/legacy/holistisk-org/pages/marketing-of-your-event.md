When you book Hjerterum, your event automatically gets good exposure. The event is published on both our website and on our Facebook page, which currently has over 6000 followers.

If you want to market your event beyond this, it is your own responsibility to do this. An effective way to reach others with your event can be, for example, by buying ads on Facebook. You can also invite your network, and share your event in various relevant groups on Facebook. We have made a list of suggestions for groups (see below). (NOTE: Read the guidelines for the group in question before sharing, to ensure that your event fits into the group.)
