#### All Online events at ‘Hjerterum’

Click a title below to read more about each individual event.

At our Hjerterum online events, we wish to offer a variety of themes suited for everyone and ranging broadly in taste. Yoga, mindfulness, meditation, support groups, sharing groups, breathwork, dreamwork, acupuncture, chanting, mantra songs can all be part of the program.  
This wide range of events all have the purpose, each with their unique approach, of letting you get in touch with the core of who you really are – an inner journey of discovery into being.

If you think you would like to attend online events regularly, it would make sense and save you money to become a member of Hjerterum if you are not already so. You can do that at [https://holistisk.org/become-a-member/#join](https://holistisk.org/become-a-member/#join)

If you have a good idea for a new event within the category of spirituality/self-development, then don’t hesitate to send us an email at [events@holistisk.org](mailto:events@holistisk.org)
