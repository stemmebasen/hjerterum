Let’s start by saying what we are **not**:

-   Not a place with ready-made answers or quick-fixes.
-   Not a closed group or sect.
-   Not tied to one specific faith, religion or political position.
-   Not a place without room for humor and lightness.

**Hjerterum is for everyone**  
Hjerterum is an open and safe space with community, creativity and personal development. With us you can participate in a lot of nourishing and meaningful events – e.g. lectures, concerts, yoga, breathwork, workshops and social events. If you are a teacher/facilitator, you are always welcome to use our place. [**Click here**](https://holistisk.org/price-list/) to read more.

**Become part of Tribes**  
You can also become part of Hjerterum Tribes – a small group that you become a part of for a while. Hjerterum Tribes exist to bring Hjerterum members together in a nourishing community. A tribe consists of between 5 and 8 members and is an opportunity to get to know yourself and other people more deeply. [**Read more about Hjerterum Tribes**](https://holistisk.org/tribes/).

Whether you are looking for peace, inspiration or new acquaintances, you are welcome. We believe that good energy and openness create positive encounters—between people and with oneself.

Welcome to a place that **gives space to be** ♡
